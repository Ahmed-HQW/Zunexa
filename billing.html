FAQ

Common questions

Can I try Zarix for free?
Yes. The Free plan gives you full access to the Q&A browser, short AI answers, global chat, and 5 @ai queries per day — no credit card required.
What exactly are "ZARIX MAX Blueprints"?
MAX Blueprints are our most detailed AI coaching responses — multi-phase periodized programs (8–16 weeks), exact set/rep/load prescriptions, nutrition protocols by training phase, supplement timing, recovery systems, and benchmark targets. They represent the full depth of the Zarix AI coaching system.
How does the @ai chat command work?
In the Global Chat, type @ai followed by any fitness question (e.g. @ai how do I improve my squat?). The AI will respond publicly with an answer tuned to your subscription tier. Max users receive full blueprints, while Free users get a concise summary.
Can I cancel at any time?
Yes. All plans are month-to-month (or annual with 30% savings). You can cancel anytime and retain access until the end of your billing period. No cancellation fees.
Is the annual plan billed upfront?
Yes. Annual plans are billed once per year at the discounted rate. You save 30% compared to monthly billing.
How do I contact the coach directly?
